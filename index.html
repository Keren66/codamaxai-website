What we do

One partner for the data and the compute behind it.

AI projects tend to stall in two places: getting enough well-labeled data, and getting the compute to train on it. We handle both, and we stay involved while the work runs.

01

Data Annotation

Human-in-the-loop labeling for training and evaluation datasets, with review built into every batch.

  • Image and video: boxes, polygons, keypoints, tracking
  • Text: classification, entities, sentiment
  • Audio: transcription and segment tagging
  • Structured and tabular data labeling
Multimodal
02

Compute Services

Managed compute capacity for training runs, fine-tuning and experiments, sized to the stage of your project.

  • Capacity for training and fine-tuning
  • Ready-to-use environments for experiments
  • Scale up for crunch periods, down afterwards
  • Monitoring of running workloads
Scalable
03

Infrastructure Support

Engineers who connect your data operations and compute into one working pipeline, and keep it running.

  • Pipeline setup and data hand-off
  • Storage and transfer between stages
  • Hands-on troubleshooting during delivery
  • Progress and quality reporting
End to end

FAQ

Common questions.

Which data types do you annotate?

Image, video, text, audio and structured data. If your data doesn't fit neatly into one of these, tell us about it and we'll scope it with you.

Can we start with a small pilot?

Yes. Most engagements begin with a pilot batch so we can agree on guidelines and quality targets before moving to larger volumes.

Can we use compute without annotation?

Yes. Compute services and infrastructure support are available on their own or together with annotation.

Where is your team based?

In Malaysia (GMT+8). We work with AI teams in the region and internationally.
